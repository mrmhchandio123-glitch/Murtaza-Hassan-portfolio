03 — Final year project

Design and performance analysis of a parabolic trough solar water heating system

My final year project aimed at utilizing solar energy to convert water into steam. I live in a hometown where summer temperatures frequently go beyond 50°C, so I wondered why we are not taking benefit of this abundant source of energy. This thought motivated me to develop the project, which I successfully completed.

Subsequently, it received a nomination for the National Incubation Center Karachi, and I also presented it at the 1st International Symposium on Climate Change, Mitigation, and Environmental Sustainability held at my university.
